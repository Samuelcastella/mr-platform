# MR עדולם — Storefront Experience v1.1

Status: IMPLEMENTED  
Surface: Public storefront  
Production: https://storefront-production-e7b5.up.railway.app/

## Purpose

The storefront must communicate MR עדולם as a scalable brand and commerce platform, not as a single-niche boutique.

## Public story now represented

- Initial physical operation in Puerto Cortés.
- Third-party products and multi-supplier purchasing.
- Evolution toward private label.
- Evolution toward MR עדולם-owned products.
- Multi-category expansion.
- Physical store + storefront + future app + commerce core.
- Marketplace model and future external channels.
- Local, regional and global sourcing.
- Control Center concept for catalog, inventory, orders, suppliers, purchasing, logistics, channels, analytics and audit.
- Principles of curation, traceability and open architecture.
- Four-stage growth path: operation → omnichannel → owned brand → expansion.

## UX rule

The public website can explain the ecosystem, but operational controls remain conceptually separate from the customer storefront and will require authentication before becoming a production administration surface.

## Technical rule

The catalog and inventory source of truth remains the commerce core/PostgreSQL. Browser-local data is transitional demo/fallback state only.
